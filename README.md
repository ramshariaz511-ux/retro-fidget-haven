# ⚡ RETRO ZEN: THE ULTIMATE ANTI-STRESS DIGITAL FIDGET HUB ⚡

A vintage, pixelated, sensory anti-stress website packed with 10 digital fidget toys, categorized for seamless relaxation. Featuring a dark grey aesthetic with glowing neon cyan accents, tactile 3D beveled buttons, and 100% procedurally synthesized audio via the Web Audio API (zero external sound files needed).

---

## 🎮 Included Fidget Toys by Category

### 1. 🌀 Fidget Spinners (`Category: Spinners`)
- **Fidget Spinner 2000**: High-precision angular physics simulation with moment of inertia, drag, and friction.
- **Controls**: Flick Clockwise (`↻`), Flick Counter-Clockwise (`↺`), Brake, or drag/swipe directly on the canvas. Mouse wheel scrolling is also supported.
- **Skins**: Classic Tri-Wing, Ninja Dual-Blade Shuriken, Neon Vortex Disc, and Clockwork Brass Cog.
- **Stats & Audio**: Real-time RPM speedometer, Peak RPM, Rotations counter, and dynamic pitch-shifting bearing whir sound.

### 2. 🔘 Buttons & Tactile Switches (`Category: Buttons & Switches`)
- **Infinite Bubble Wrap 2.0**: Grid of tactile bubbles. Click or drag across the sheet to pop with randomized acoustic pitch. Click "Unpop All" for a fresh sheet.
- **Mechanical Switch Tester Box**: 4 authentic switch varieties side by side:
  - *Gateron Oil King*: Deep creamy, lubed linear **THOCK**.
  - *Cherry MX Blue*: Crisp sharp high-frequency **CLICK**.
  - *Holy Panda*: Tactile double-bump **SNAP**.
  - *Boba U4T*: Muted velvet **SILENT**.
- **The Forbidden Big Red Button**: Yellow/black industrial hazard styling with a hinged flip-cover you must open before pressing. Triggers screen-shake, pixel confetti explosions, and humorous retro Zen messages.
- **Heavy Aircraft Toggle Matrix**: 6 vintage metal toggle levers with status indicator LEDs.

### 3. ⚙️ Gears & Clockwork (`Category: Gears & Clockwork`)
- **Interlocking Gear Train**: Realistic tooth-meshing physics where turning one gear propagates through the entire chain with correct mechanical gear ratios.
- **Motor Drive**: Toggle automated motor drive with speed control slider.
- **Presets**: Classic 4-Gear Chain and High-Speed Step-Up Ratio.
- **Sound**: Rhythmic mechanical tick-clacks synchronized to gear tooth movement.

### 4. ⌨️ Typing Gadgets (`Category: Typing Gadgets`)
- **Vintage 1940s Typewriter**:
  - Mechanical platen carriage that advances with every typed keystroke.

  - Functional carriage return lever with ratchet zip audio and paper feed.
  - Ribbon color toggle (Antique Black vs. Carmine Red) and paper tear button.
  - Supports physical keyboard input and on-screen round keys.
- **60% Custom Mechanical Keyboard Playground**:
  - Full pixel keyboard with custom keycaps.

  - Dynamic RGB underglow modes (Cyan Zen Glow, Rainbow Wave, Ripple on Press).
  - Real-time keystroke counter, WPM gauge, and typing stream preview.
